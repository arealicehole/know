## Competitive structure vs Frank (analyst view)

```
                    Productized agents (A)
                           ↑
     AI receptionist $179–500/mo  ·  setup $2.5–7.5k one-shot
                           |
     Frank AB unique zone: agents + fractional tech + print perk
                           |
     MSP $100–250/user     ·   Break-fix $100–150/hr   ·   Print quote-only
                           ↓
                    Wallet owners today
```

**No pure clone found** in public AZ market that openly sells *agent systems + tech retainer + print broker perk* as one stack. Closest substitutes are **split wallets**: MSP or break-fix for B; AI agency for thin A; AlphaGraphics/PRI for P.
