# B2B SaaS / Local Service Marketing Craft — Internal Playbook Notes (2025-2026)

Objective: distilled, original notes for an internal playbook for a multi-client marketing agent. No invented stats — all named numbers cite sources.
Subjects: offer/value equation, narrow ICP, funnel/channel logic, short-form video craft, retention/referrals, one-KPI experiments, objection handling/fast follow-up, 90-day prioritization.
Source basis: web search 2025-2026; see evidence index. Sources marked (vendor) where marketing vendor blogs are the only source — treat as directional.

## 1) Offer / value equation + risk reversal
Hormozi value equation: Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort/Sacrifice). Raise dream outcome, raise perceived likelihood, cut time delay, cut effort — and price up rather than discounting.
Sources: alexhormozi.wiki/frameworks/value-equation-explained; convios.com/en/methods/value-equation; theexecutiveinsight.com/articles/hormozi-value-equation; mmsvegas.com/library/value-equation-explained

Risk reversal (guarantee framework, Hart 1988 / Spence signaling):
- Guarantee only convinces if honoring it is expensive for a poor provider — a cheap-to-honor guarantee signals nothing.
- Quality test before any commitment: controllable? measurable? meaningful? affordable (<$720/120 orders example at 98% on-time; ~$3.6k at 90%)? simple to invoke and collect?
- Commitment types in B2B: service commitment (e.g. 4h response), deadline guarantee (credit), satisfaction guarantee (free rework to acceptance criteria), results guarantee (highest risk, only where you control the outcome).
- 4 types of risk-reversal: unconditional (money back), conditional (conditions = the behaviors of successful customers), anti-guarantee (assertively none), performance-linked (payment tied to outcome).
- Wording: specific outcome, specific date, specific consequence, one sentence, placed at point of risk — never buried in small print.
Sources: owners-council.com/en/blog/offering-guarantees-b2b/; convios.com/en/methods/guarantees-risk-reversal; copywrite.now/blog/risk-reversal-b2b-copywriting; resources.rework.com/libraries/deal-closing/risk-concerns; cs3technology.com/resources/articles/money-back-guarantee-whats-the-deal

Practical craft: short paid pilot / proof-of-concept (30-60 days, success criteria up front, convert on success); month-to-month / no-penalty cancellation instead of annual lock; outcome guarantee for sales cycles where control/proof exist. Anti-patterns: vague conditions nobody qualifies for; "satisfaction" guarantees with no consequence; costly results guarantees you cannot control; hiding caps from buyers (builds distrust + call-center complaints — see lifecycle-leaders.hightouch.com).

## 2) Narrow ICP and segment sizing
Key ideas: ICP = manageable subset of target market, not a category definition. Narrow enough that you can name ~20 accounts and explain why; if it includes most of your market, it is not an ICP.
Source: rivexs.com/guides/saas-icp (9 attributes: firmographics, technographics, business-model fit, pain in buyer's language, triggers, buying conditions, disqualifiers, stakeholder map, scoring + segmentation + testing).

Operationalization:
- Five-question framework (a16z): which customers get most out of it? traits of best customers? recurring objections? easiest to upsell and why? your competitors' customers? Triangulate top cohort on LTV/revenue growth AND speed to purchase AND speed to growth.
- Clearbit case: narrowed from broad ICP to LTV ICP — 18% of leads drove ~86% of long-term revenue; narrowing improved economics. (vendor citation)
- Tiered ICP: Tier A primary (best economics, focus roadmap/outbound), Tier B acceptable (lower ACV, self-serve), Tier C opportunistic (inbound, economics reasonable). Keep 1-3 tiers, not 10 personas.
- Operationalize: assign an owner for target-account list, map >=3 stakeholders per account, align MQL/SQL/AQO definitions, identify buying triggers, define high-value offers, encode in ops systems.
- Scoring: weighted fit x trigger strength; simple is fine.
- Testing: outbound reply quality, objection type, meeting-to-opportunity conversion are fastest ICP tests (weeks).
Sources: a16z.com/framework-define-refine-icp/; clearbit.com/resources/books/ideal-customer-profile/redefining-icp; getmonetizely.com/articles/how-to-create-an-ideal-customer-profile-icp-for-your-saas-startup-that-actually-drives-revenue; scalevp.com/resources/10x-your-go-to-market-strategy-with-a-sharper-icp-90b8c

"Do not track who fits; track fit x economics x speed." Multi-client playbook rule: per client, log Tier-A/B/C counts and win-rate/ACV/speed for each; review quarterly.

## 3) Funnel / channel logic — B2B supply, local print, trade shows
Where buyers actually learn (2025-2026):
- Independent publishers, peers and events drive initial awareness. 2025 study: 6% banner, 15% ChatGPT, 17% peer-review/G2/Reddit, 17% social, 26% LinkedIn, 29% webinar, 31% analyst content, 32% vendor outreach, 33% virtual event/trade show, 36% industry events, 36% professional orgs, 39/40% vendor sites, 49% Google — for "first awareness". (vendor survey, directional)
- Content discovery (Redpoint Insights 2025): LinkedIn 46%, analyst firm / vendor's own website / search / industry media / syndicated email each ~38-39%, peer shares/community/Slack ~22%, email each.
- Reddit x SurveyMonkey (n=1,202, Dec 2025-Jan 2026): search engines 57%, vendor sites 52%, peer recommendation 31%? — actually peer recs 52% top use case "capabilities 53/pricing 53/compat 47". 77% seek social proof on Reddit; video demos (32% very valuable) > user testimonials (37% very valuable) > community discussions (27%) > analyst (27%) > one-sheets (17%).
- Manufacturing/B2B (Blake / vendor study): vendor/supplier websites 85%, technical trade publications, conferences/trade shows, peer recs 45%, sales/application engineers 44%, engineering forums, webinars, YouTube 12% LinkedIn 11%, gen-AI 12% → trust-heavy channels dominate complex buys.
- Local search + reviews: local SEO, Google business profile, reviews (email is preferred for review requests; a reminder can double response; email > SMS > in-app; Google preferred platform).
- Trade-show specifics (WebFX): use digital forms to expedite sign-ups (no data entry), geofence attendees after event, segment nurture drips, highlight certifications/USPs at booth.
- Print: direct mail/repeat schedule (skip holidays/vacations), QR code + landing page, review cards w/ QR to Google reviews (example: 35→90 reviews in 9 months), catalogs/brochures, door hangers.

Channel logic for the playbook:
- Local print/local service: found-ness (Google Business Profile + location pages + pricing/FAQ + case notes) + reviews flywheel + trade-show capture + referral loop + print/display reinforcement. Don't overestimate LinkedIn/gen-AI for complex-service buyers — trust and vendor-site depth matter.
- B2B supply/SaaS: LinkedIn + peer/review/community content + search SEO for consideration; vendor site as primary deep-dive; LinkedIn personal posts > company page noise.
- Trade shows: booth + landing page + geofenced nurture + same-week follow-up (see objection section).

## 4) Creative hooks, thumbnails, proof-driven short-form
Short-form for B2B (2025) (vendor sources, treat as directional):
- Platform mix: LinkedIn for awareness/trust, YouTube Shorts for search-led discovery, Instagram/TikTok for awareness; 15-60s.
- Hook in the first 3 seconds (most drop-off; pattern is claim + proof or pain).
- Proof-driven: customer logos, real results numbers, case fragments, before/after, raw b-roll ("real, not polished"), creator-style (someone talking on camera vs slide-deck).
- Thumbnails: clarity, contrast, consistent template, readable text, one clear subject — clarity + contrast + a face/visual hook.
- Social proof stack: 4-second hook -> proof point -> CTA -> subtle branded outro.
- B2B video in 2025: authentic over polished; creator-led vs branded; teach/show/result shape.
Sources: oktopost.com/blog/short-form-video-b2b-marketing-2025/; motionvillee.com 23 trends; contently.com/2025/10/08/...; usevisuals.com thumbnail design; contentbuck.com/blog/thumbnail-design; forbes.com/councils/forbescommunicationscouncil/2025/09/15/19-expert-tips-for-leveraging-short-form-video-for-b2b-success/

## 5) Retention, reorders, referrals
NRR/retention benchmarks (2025 SaaS Capital, n>1,000 private B2B SaaS): median gross revenue retention 91%, median net revenue retention 101% (vendor citation, current).
Source: saas-capital.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/RB32WS1-2025-B2B-SaaS-Retention-Benchmarks.pdf; planhat.com/customer-success/customer-retention; joinpavilion.com benchmarks; wudpecker.io

Referral/repeat craft:
- Review cadence: earn 24-48h after emotional/high-effort services; wait until results evaluated for time-delay services; same-day/next-day after quick transactional ones; Google is preferred platform (email best channel); a well-timed reminder can double response.
- Referral offers (raffle entry/discount on next service, e.g. home services); referral partnerships with nearby non-competing businesses; mutual-promotion swaps; review cards (QR); showcase best testimonials/photos/videos on money pages.
- Local print: direct mail repeated; calendar-drip/segmentation by location/service/size; seasonal maintenance reorders via drip campaigns.
- Customer retention is offense: relationship of new-sale bookings to revenue retention.
Sources: searchengineland.com/guide/local-marketing; webfx.com; groundleads.com; littlemountainprinting.com; lairedigital.com

## 6) One-KPI experiments and growth loops
Playbook rules:
- One primary metric per experiment + guardrails (unsub rate, page-load, deliveryability, CAC, retention).
- ICE prioritization (impact x confidence x ease) for what to test.
- 70/20/10 split (70 proven / 20 promising / 10 wildcards).
- Hypothesis: "If we [change], then [metric] improves by [lift] because [insight]."
- Sample size, randomization, no early-stopping (collect full sample; significance needs time).
- Offer tests need guardrails: cap discounts (e.g. 50% off, max savings $20), be upfront with caps (burying caps causes complaints and cancellations); capped discount > uncapped 50%.
- Long-term metrics: downstream pipeline/revenue, retention, LTV — not clicks.
- Growth loops: ICP/offer/channel loop -> leads -> activation -> referral (review -> new lead) -> retention -> expansion -> upsell. Loops outperform funnels when loops are instrumented (self-referral loops, review-referral loop, referral-swap loop, content-loop).
Sources: amplitude.com/explore/experiment/marketing-experimentation; growthrocks.com/blog/growth-hacking-playbook/; aakashg.com/a-b-testing-best-practices/; pedowitzgroup.com; lifecycle-leaders.hightouch.com; joinpavilion.com

## 7) Objection handling + fast follow-up
- Identify objection type before replying; 48h follow-up window maximizes engagement for timing objections (vendor claim, directional).
- Risk-concern may mask real objection (e.g. "not sure" = disengagement, not risk; overpowering risk = stall tactics). Ask: "If we address [risk] via [solution], does that resolve hesitation?"
- Best responses: acknowledge -> reframe -> demonstrate value -> invite next action; specific proof + deadline/timing anchors (e.g. "if we don't move by Mar 1 we'll miss the date").
- Fast follow-up: 24-48h after discovery; two specific times + booking link in replies; reminders + one-click reschedule.
- Local trades: trade-show leads same-week follow-up; review/trust-fast for local buyers.
Sources: sparkco.ai/blog/create-objection-handling-playbook; highspot.com/en-gb/blog/objection-handling/; growleads.io/blog/blog-sales-objection-handling-b2b-revenue/; blog.sellible.ai/objection-handling-playbook-for-b2b-sales/; resources.rework.com/libraries/deal-closing/risk-concerns; groundleads.com

## 8) 90-day prioritization + what to avoid
90-day playbook skeleton (vendor sources, directional):
- Days 1-30: audit (IC? ICP fit, funnels, one-KPI baseline, current CRM); define 1 KPI (e.g. pipeline/bookings/Qualified events); set 70/20/10 experiment budget.
- Days 31-60: activate (outbound/LinkedIn + proof-driven short-form + nurture/review loop + trade-show capture).
- Days 61-90: automate + scale winners (capped discount offers, referral loop, monthly experiment cadence).
- Lead gen vs trust-building split; 1/3 retention work (reviews, reorders, referral); 2/3 acquisition.
- Avoid: broad ICP, uncapped discounting, no-guardrail experiments, ignoring retention signals, over-invest in LinkedIn/AI for complex purchases, trust-only-dedicated "refined" plans, one-off campaigns, chasing vanity metrics, sharing learnings only internally.
Sources: noobpreneur.com/2025/11/01/the-90-day-gtm-playbook...; jam7.com/blog/90-day-marketing-plan-template-b2b-tech; saashero.net/strategy/best-b2b-saas-gtm-strategy/; productiveshop.com/resources/blog/cmo-first-90-days-playbook/; wedigtech.com/resources/insights/how-to-use-ai-for-marketing-90-day-playbook-b2b-saas

## Multi-client playbook additions
- Per-client KPI dashboard: ICP Tier A/B/C count, win-rate, LTV:CAC, NRR, review velocity, referral count, ICP-fit-driven outreach reply rate.
- Experiment log: one KPI, guardrails, hypothesis, decision rule, results — reuse across clients.
- Risk-reversal inventory: per client, 1-2 guarantees the client controls (deadline/response/satisfaction), with published rules.
- Trade-show motion: capture -> segment -> geofence -> 7-day follow-up.
