## Objective
Rewrite the Jim Bob vertical playbook for owner Jason at American Screen Supply — local/national B2B supply of screen printing, direct-to-film (DTF), UV print, and garment decoration consumables/equipment — covering who buys, discovery channels, seasonality, trust signals, objections, converting channels, 5 marketing plays, claims to avoid, and 5 competitor-adjacent shops by region with positioning gaps.

## Summary
The B2B screen-printing/DTF supply industry (2024–2026) is a regional-and-national mix of independent suppliers (often founders-as-owners, e.g. American Screen Supply in Scottsdale AZ), national wholesale DTF/UV transfer houses, equipment/consumables vendors, and trade-show-driven relationships. Buyers are shop owners and production managers (not individual hobbyists), discovered via YouTube how-to channels, Facebook/WhatsApp apparel-industry groups, trade shows (Impressions Expo), Google/local search, and word of mouth. Q4 (Aug–Dec) is the peak rush driven by holiday/corporate/event decoration; Q1–Q2 is the slow season when shops set reorder programs and reviews. Trust wins on consistency, on-time delivery, honest specs, and repeat-order economics; objections cluster on price, stock depth, film/print quality, and warranty. The plays that convert: educate (video demos), reorder programs with artwork-on-file, email lifecycle/reorder reminders, Google Business Profile + reviews, and video demos. Claims to avoid: unverified "the best" superlatives, invented certifications, "guaranteed" wash counts/durability, unverified pricing.

## Who buys
- **Shop owners / decorators** (screen printers, DTF shops, embroiderers, hat/promotional businesses) resell or press DTF transfers, UV stickers, screen-printed transfers, patches, and consumables.
- **Production managers / buyers** at larger decorated-apparel contractors and promotional product distributors who need volume, gang sheets, and tiered reorder pricing.
- **Online/Etsy/Shopify sellers and boutique brands** who need 25-item minimums and whitespace.
- **Embroidery-adjacent buyers** adding UV transfers or fauxbroidery to their menu without owning DTF equipment.

## Seasonality
- **Q4 peak:** holiday/corporate/event decoration rush drives 75%-or-more of order volume; planning begins ~July-Aug; shipping cutoffs (USPS ~Nov 21 for Christmas delivery) mean order-in deadlines; blanks and popular sizes sell out; "black XL hoodies" warnings.
- **Slow season:** Q1-Q2, decorators use it for inventory, reviews, and reorder setups; SODA Winter 2025 report notes 137 decorated-apparel firms with tariff-driven cost inflation squeezing margins (H2 2025 flat).
- **Impressions Expo (Jan):** resets supplier conversations.

Evidence: graphics-pro.com/article/the-holiday-rush-should-not-be-a-surprise/, impressionsmagazine.com/build-your-business/planning-for-the-holiday-season-custom-apparel-decorating-rush/169943/, impressionsmagazine.com/build-your-business/super-size-your-sales-this-holiday-season/39276/, printing.org SODA Winter 2025 executive summary PDF, apparelist.com Nov 7 2025 (heat-transfer + embroidery + screen printing are most used; ~3 methods in-house, 2 outsourced).

## Trust signals that win deals
- **Consistency / repeatable specs** (spec honesty: sizes, printable areas, 300dpi artwork spec, 2pt line weights, transparent backgrounds) — linked to commercial trust.
- **On-time delivery & same-day shipping by noon**; quoted lead times (2-5 day standard, rush same-day) honored.
- **Blind / white-label shipping** — order arrives looking like it came from the shop (DD Creative Co, Kode Garment).
- **Repeat-order economics:** artwork-on-file reorders skip proofing; tiered pricing (25+/100+/200+); subscription/billing tiers (Long Island DTF $5/mo tiers); no-minimums on most SKUs.
- **In-house production / owned equipment** (production not outsourced) — "we own the printers."
- **Proof/spec transparency** (per-sq-in pricing, art-file requirements).
- **Honesty on reprints / warranties** (TX DTF Wholesale Prints: checked quality, reprint on issue).

Evidence: linkedin.com/pulse/why-consistency-builds-trust-commercial-printing-spectraprint-twjnf (2026), sourceday.com/blog/supplier-reliability/ (2026).

## Common objections
- **Price** — lowest-cost national suppliers (long island $0.06/sq in, usadtftransfers, goldendtf, dtftown) compete on per-unit pricing; Kode Garment $0.03/sq in DTF undercuts many.
- **Stock depth** — holiday-season shortage of black XL hoodies, popular sizes; supply chain/tariffs (SODA Winter 2025).
- **Film/print quality** — operators compare DTF film quality, adhesive powder, underbase fidelity; gang-sheet alignment issues.
- **Warranty / reprint policy** — shops ask who re-does failed runs; DD Creative reprints only on proof approved defects; Long Island doesn't advertise one; HYI claims 30-day guarantee on UV transfers (China maker).
- **Minimums / MOQ** — DD Creative 25+; Long Island no minimums; Kode none; HYI 10pcs UV.
- **Artwork requirements** — 300dpi, transparent background, fonts outlined, 2pt line weight minimums (Kode; DD Creative).

Evidence: lassosupplychain.com (supplier performance 2026), sourceday.com (reliability 2026), dtfdatabase.com (Arizona DTF suppliers), hyifashion.com UV transfer FAQ (wash tests 50+, SGS/CPSIA claims).

- **Trade shows** — hands-on demos, exclusives, in-booth sampling (Impressions Expo, Jan 22-24 2026).

## 5 marketing plays Jim Bob should own
1. **Educate/show** — weekly YouTube/short-form video demos of press settings, film/print quality, reorder proofing; address operator questions in DTF/ScreenPrinting Facebook groups (not spam; genuine advice).
2. **Reorder / standing-order program** — artwork-on-file, skip proofing, tiered pricing (25/100/200+), blind/white-label shipping; highlight in-house production.
3. **Email lifecycle** — Q4 pre-rush order-in deadline reminders ("order in before Nov 21 to hit Christmas delivery"), post-rush reorder waves (Jan return visit).
4. **GBP + reviews** — Scottsdale-local reviews, case-study testimonials from Arizona/national shop owners, weekly "supplier to the trade" post.
5. **Video demo / show the machine** — press timing, gang-sheet alignment, reprint policy; matched to operator pain points (yellow-label: honest specs).

## What NOT to claim
- Unverified “the best” or “largest” superlatives (American Screen Supply claims "Voted Best" — source unclear; do not amplify).
- Invented certifications (SGS / CPSIA / Pony claims on HYI/UVDTF) unless verified.
- Guaranteed wash counts, "unlimited" color, or 100% durability claims.
- "Same-day" promises without measured lead time; or per-unit "% off" figures not in writing.
- Fake review volumes or fake testimonials.

1. **Arizona (near American Screen Supply)** — Buckets of Ink (Scottsdale supplies, van delivery to Tempe); Arizona Screen Printer (Scottsdale, full-color DTF); Custom Transfers (Scottsdale DTF). Gap: local specialists sell DTF/equipment but lack national wholesale/gang-sheet programs and reorder economics — American Screen Supply can win on national scale + in-house DTF + blind shipping.
2. **National (national wholesale DTF/UV)** — Long Island DTF (NY, $0.06/sq in, UV DTF, subscriptions, $99+ free shipping); DTF Superstore (largest US supplier claim, 2007, education + bootcamps); Discovery Lancer (DTF wholesale, free-ship $350+); Golden DTF (national wholesale, UV DTF, raised/foil, tiers); Kode Garment (Canada, no minimums, $0.03 DTF/$0.06 UV, Montreal production, 2-5d). 3. **East Tennessee (national DTF)** — DD Creative Co (East TN, 25+ MOQ, blind/white-label, art-on-file reorders, in-house production, same-day quotes). Gap: small-shop scale, 2-3d production, no subscription tiers — local AZ shop can win on speed/local delivery but must match reorder-file economics.
4. **Houston / Dallas (South Central)** — DTF Wholesale Prints (Houston TX, same-day-noon ship, quality reprint policy). Gap: small-shop scale, no UV specialty/line — American Screen Supply can win on UV + gang-sheet + equipment coverage.
5. **Monroe NC (Southeast)** — USColorworks (Monroe NC, DTF/UV/contract decoration, 1-2 day shipping, 150-person crew, 50k sq ft). Gap: bulk contract fulfillment vs. local: American Screen Supply should not chase bulk apparel contract work; instead own local/national B2B supplies + AZ delivery + honest specs.
