# How to List a New Cryptocurrency on CoinGecko

Last updated 1 month ago

Before applying, please ensure you meet our important prerequisites. First, your cryptocurrency must be actively tradable on a cryptocurrency exchange tracked by CoinGecko; you can view our supported markets on our [Exchange page](https://www.coingecko.com/en/exchanges).

Additionally, please do not ask your community to spam CoinGecko with "When list?" inquiries, as repeated spam will result in your coin or token being disqualified from listing.

### **Step-by-Step Submission Guide**

To submit your token or coin for listing, please follow these simple steps:

1. **Log In:** Log in to your CoinGecko account. If you do not have an account, you will need to sign up first. You can read this article, _\[_ [_How do I sign up for a CoinGecko account?_](https://support.coingecko.com/hc/en-us/articles/7248470874649-How-do-I-sign-up-for-a-CoinGecko-account) _\]_, for further information.
