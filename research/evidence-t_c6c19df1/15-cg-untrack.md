# Why can't I find this coin/token listing anymore?

Last updated 1 month ago

Please note that we periodically review existing listings, and if they fail to meet our requirements, then they will be deactivated or removed.

Do note that the criteria listed on our [Methodology](https://www.coingecko.com/en/methodology) page are NOT exhaustive.

At CoinGecko, we evaluate each token listing using an internal set of criteria, which includes liquidity, team presence, project maturity, as well as several other evaluation factors that are undisclosed.
