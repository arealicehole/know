### 2. Industry verification (hot-environment beverage ads)

**Cold cues drive thirst and quality perception**
- Visible condensation, frosted glass, ice cubes act as subconscious cues for freshness and quality; advertisers use ice/frost imagery to trigger satisfaction (Storific, 2025).  
- Warm, crowded, or stimulating environments increase demand for cold drinks; presentation that *looks* colder increases appeal before tasting (Storific).  
- Practical photo craft: glycerin/water condensation, freezer pre-chill, ice (real/acrylic), frosted glass, cold marble/snow/misty fridge backgrounds (Fotokatalog; Pebblely; Squareshot).

**Show the product hard**
- Winning beverage ads always show pack front + liquid; bright backgrounds; legible overlays; hierarchy = product/flavor first, benefit badge + CTA next (Quimby Digital beverage advertising playbook, 2025/2026).  
- Sensory close-ups and authentic serving scenarios outperform abstract product-only or pure lifestyle without product (Top Growth Marketing F&B guide).  
- Clear bottles that showcase juice color signal freshness/purity; color-coded flavor differentiation is standard cold-press branding (BottleStore cold-pressed branding).

**Color psychology for thirst + juice**
- Cool colors (blue/green/teal) associate with calm, hydration, refreshment; warm fruit colors (orange/yellow/red/berry) signal flavor and appetite on the *product*, not the whole field (Moxie Sozo beverage color psychology; Forty8Creates / Charles Spence: color is the primary intrinsic cue for expected taste).  
- Implication for hot-climate flyers: **cool field + warm liquid** = heat relief + flavor promise. Teal negative space (MAZA choice) is correct.

**Messaging that sells drinks**
- Lead with **flavor + function + format** specifics (“Peach Mango – 12-oz – cold-pressed”) — vague “refreshes you!” underperforms (Quimby).  
- Occasion mapping matters: post-workout, midday heat, drive/queue, outdoor labor, family mobile-eat — map ONDARUN to **AZ heat relief + grab-and-go mobile food**.  
- Premium lines: mute heavy discount talk; lead with craft/sourcing/cold-pressed; still show clear prices for mobile-food decision speed.

**Cold-pressed category visual codes**
- Tall clear PET or glass, vibrant liquid color, minimal clean type, vertical labels, “clean ingredients” aesthetic (Suja, Pressed, Greenhouse references via BottleStore).  
- 72% of U.S. consumers say packaging design influences purchase (BusinessDasher via BottleStore) — flyer must look like packaging-quality, not coupon-mailer.

- Right or lower-left **cool teal negative space** plate: flavor list + prices + QR  
- Bottom 10%: URL + Queen Creek AZ + fine print

### Shared production rules (both versions)

1. **Leave real text to post** — generate clean plates; composite brand/prices/QR in design tool for crisp print type.  
2. **3:1 product:environment** visual weight.  
3. **Never warm-grade the liquid** — keep juice saturated and “cold.”  
4. **Labels readable if possible**; if gen blurs labels, plan real label overlays.  
5. **QR safe zone** ≥ 1" from trim, high contrast.  
6. **Print:** 300 dpi, 0.125" bleed, CMYK proof; watch teal shift in CMYK.  
7. **Claim hygiene:** “cold-pressed” OK if true; avoid disease/medical claims.
